Brief

A personal AI daily briefing.

Role
Design & development

  • 5things worth your time each morning. Or 3, or 8: you set the length.
  • ListenThe brief reads itself aloud, one story after another.
  • Swiperight to keep a story, left to skip it for today.
  • WhyEvery story can explain why it was picked, using only what you did on this phone.

Try it · live demo with sample data, nothing is sent
  1. 01Tap a story to read it, then tap "Why am I seeing this?"
  2. 02Swipe a story right to save it, or left to skip it
  3. 03Tap the S to change the typeface, length and appearance
  4. 04Open Interests and drag a topic line

A pipeline gathers several sources covering the same event and merges them into one short story, written like a friend who gets tech: what happened, why it matters, how it works, what you can build and what to watch.

The iOS app ranks the stories by how much you care about each topic, and learns from what you read, save and skip.
