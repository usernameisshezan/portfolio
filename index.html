    About

    I'm Shezan. I like software that gets out of your way.

    The things I build tend to be small and specific: a calmer way to read the news, a better way to choose where to eat, a private place to leave a note for one person. I design them, build them, and keep sanding until they need fewer buttons than they started with.

    I work across the whole thing. Native iOS in SwiftUI, Android in Compose, Flutter when one codebase makes more sense, and the web in Next.js. Behind the screens I write the pipelines too: Python that gathers the sources, and language models that turn them into something worth reading.

    What I care about most is the part you feel more than see. A swipe that lands. A haptic at the right moment. A screen that is ready before you think to wait for it.
